Key design decisions

Why 10 cores instead of Willene's 8? Willene's Basic wheel has: Happy, Calm, Loved, Surprised, Scared, Sad, Angry, Guilty. The app adds Disgusted, Bad, and Fearful (more clinical), and promotes Shame, Guilt, Love to first-class cores following the DBT model. "Calm" is absorbed into Happy via the Peaceful and Trusting mid children.

Why are Shame and Guilt separate cores? DBT Handout 6 gives both equal clinical weight to Anger and Fear, each with their own prompting events, biological changes, and opposite actions. The Willene Basic PDF states explicitly: "Guilt says: I did something wrong. Shame says: Something is wrong with me."

DBT Envy and Jealousy? DBT Handout 6 lists both as primary emotions. Jealous appears as a specific under Mad (Angry). Envy is not currently in the app — it is a candidate for a future seed pass.
