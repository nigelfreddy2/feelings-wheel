## What was asked

## Sources read

- `source/Feelings Wheel (Basic).pdf` — Willene Freddy "Name It To Tame It" worksheet: 8 cores (Happy, Calm, Loved, Surprised, Scared, Sad, Angry, Guilty), each with 4-5 mid children
- `source/Feelings Wheel SocialWorkersToolbox.pdf` — alternative wheel with similar structure

## Hierarchy designed

### Love (colour #F4A7B9)
Sources: DBT Handout 6 love words (adoration, caring, fondness, tenderness, compassion, kindness, passion, warmth) + Willene wheel (included, accepted, cared for, trusted, close)

Mid children (4):
- Caring → Compassionate, Nurturing
- Romantic → Passionate, Infatuated
- Tender → Warm, Cherished
- Grateful → Appreciative, Blessed

### Guilt (colour #B5CFD8)
Sources: DBT Handout 6 guilt words (culpability, remorse, apologetic, regret, sorry) + Willene wheel (ashamed, embarrassed, regretful, sorry, awkward)

Mid children (4):
- Remorseful → Sorry, Contrite
- Regretful → Wistful, Rueful
- Apologetic → Repentant, Accountable
- Self-Blaming → Culpable, Feeling Bad

### Shame (colour #C7B99A)
Sources: DBT Handout 6 shame words (embarrassment, mortification, humiliation, self-conscious, culpability, contrition, discomposure, shyness)

Mid children (4):
- Humiliated → Mortified, Degraded
- Embarrassed → Self-Conscious, Awkward
- Exposed → Transparent, Vulnerable
- Unworthy → Defective, Unlovable
