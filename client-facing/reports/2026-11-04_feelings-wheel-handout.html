Things to ask yourself

  • Where do I notice this in my body?
  • What happened just before I felt this way?
  • How big is this feeling — 1 to 10?
  • What might help me right now?

Guilt vs Shame
Guilt says: "I did something wrong."
Shame says: "Something is wrong with me."
Guilt can help us make things right.
Shame needs kindness, not blame.

Name it to
Tame it

Naming a feeling activates the prefrontal cortex and calms the amygdala response. You don't have to fix it — just name it.
